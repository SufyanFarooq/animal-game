About AnimalGame

What Is AnimalGame (ANT)?

AnimalGame is a decentralized, blockchain-powered gaming ecosystem with play-to-earn elements, NFTs, and staking rewards. Its first release is a 3D/4D AI battle game, where players unlock animal characters (Cat, Rat, Dog, Panda, Gorilla, Rabbit, Wolf, Lion), upgrade their abilities, and earn coins through battles.

AnimalGame is built on blockchain with scalable NFT integration, ensuring fast, gas-efficient, and transparent gameplay. Players participate in AnimalGame by purchasing animal characters with AnimalToken (ANT). Each character provides a fixed number of in-game coins, which are used in battles. Rewards are distributed daily among:

  • All holders of that character
  • Active players (both winners and losers)

This ensures sustainability, fairness, and continuous engagement.

Tokenomics & Ecosystem

Three-Token Economy

$ANT (Governance & Main Token)

  • ERC-20 token on Ethereum
  • Used for governance, staking, marketplace transactions
  • 100% community-owned, no pre-mines
  • Total Supply: 1 Billion

Plasma Points (Utility/XP Token)

  • Earned by staking $ANT
  • Required for minting new animal NFTs
  • Non-tradable, gameplay-only

UAP (Utility & Breeding Token)

  • Needed for breeding new animals
  • Can be earned via quests, battles, and achievements
  • Exchangeable for other tokens like $ANT

Example Earning Model

If 10 users buy Cat NFT ($10 each = 500 coins) → Total 5000 coins.

20 battles = 1000 coins pool/day.

50% (500 coins) → All Cat holders
35% (350 coins) → Winners
15% (150 coins) → Losers

Rewards are shared fairly among holders and active players, powered by the staking and auto grid systems.
